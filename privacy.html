This website

The site has no accounts and no analytics or advertising cookies. If you use the contact form, it opens your email app, and we receive only what you send us. We use it to reply to you. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when pages load.

Simple Panchang

Stored on your device

Your name, photo, birth date and time, selected location and app settings are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. We run no server for this app and do not receive this data. You can erase it with "Delete my data" in Account Settings.

Location and third-party services

To work out your Panchang, the app uses your GPS location if you allow it, or a place you search for. To get the place name, sunrise times and search results, it sends coordinates or the text you type to BigDataCloud and Open-Meteo. These services have their own privacy policies. You can decline GPS and choose a place manually.

Help queries

"Need Help, Submit a Query" opens your email app. If you send it, we receive the email address and text you include.
